Rohit Sharma

Rohit Sharma is one of the most popular and successful cricketers in India. He was born on 30 April 1987 in Nagpur, Maharashtra. He is known for his elegant batting style and powerful shots, which have earned him the nickname “Hitman.” Rohit has represented India in Test, One Day International (ODI), and T20 cricket and has scored many memorable centuries for the country.

Rohit Sharma has also been a successful captain of the Indian cricket team and the Mumbai Indians franchise in the Indian Premier League (IPL). He has won several IPL titles and holds the record for the highest individual score in ODI cricket, 264 runs. His calm leadership and ability to perform under pressure have made him one of the finest cricketers in the history of Indian cricket.
